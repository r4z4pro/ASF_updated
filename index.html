🔤 Pronouns — 10 Types
10 Types — Remember: P-R-D-I-Re-Re-In-Em-Re-Re:
1️⃣ Personal — I, You, He, She, It, We, They
2️⃣ Possessive — Mine, Yours, His, Hers, Ours
3️⃣ Reflexive — Myself, Yourself, Himself
4️⃣ Demonstrative — This, That, These, Those
5️⃣ Interrogative — Who, Whom, Which, What
6️⃣ Relative — Who, Which, That, Whose
7️⃣ Indefinite — Someone, Anyone, Nobody
8️⃣ Emphatic — I myself, He himself
9️⃣ Reciprocal — Each other (2 log), One another (3+)
🔟 Distributive — Each, Every, Either, Neither
📝 Pronouns — Important Rules
"Who" aur "Whom" mein fark kya hai?▶
✅ Who = Subjective case (karta)
✅ Whom = Objective case (mafool)
📌 Misal: Who did this? / Whom did you meet?
"Each other" aur "One another" ka farq?▶
✅ Each other = sirf 2 logon ke liye
✅ One another = 3 ya zyada logon ke liye
Positive sentence mein 3 persons ka order kya hota hai?▶
✅ 2nd, 3rd, 1st person
📌 Misal: You, he and I are friends.
⚠️ Apology mein pehle 1st: "I and he are sorry"
Reflexive pronoun kaunse verb ke saath nahi aata?▶
✅ Hide — "He hid himself" galat hai
📌 Aur: keep, wash, shave (in ke saath reflexive aksar nahi chahiye)
Possessive pronoun ke baad noun aata hai ya nahi?▶
✅ Nahi aata — Possessive pronoun khud hi poora kaam karta hai
📌 Misal: "This is mine" (mine ke baad kuch nahi)
📌 "My" ke baad noun aata hai: "This is my book"
